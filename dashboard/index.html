Key Executive Findings & Action Plan

1. 80/20 Customer Concentration

Just 1,315 customers (Champions + Loyal) produce 72.97% of total company revenue. Dedicated VIP tier and early access programs are recommended to prevent attrition.

2. At-Risk High Value Alert

450 high-spending customers (£1.09M past spend) are in the "At Risk" segment with >90 days since their last visit. An automated automated win-back discount email can recover an estimated ~15-20%.

3. Cohort Retention Decay

Customer retention drops sharply from 100% in Month 0 to ~32-38% in Month 1. Introducing a 14-day post-purchase re-order incentive will flatten this steep early cliff.
